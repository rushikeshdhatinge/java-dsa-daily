# FizzBuzz

**Topic:** math

## Problem
Given N, print the numbers from 1 to N. For multiples of 3 print Fizz, for multiples of 5 print Buzz, and for multiples of both print FizzBuzz.

## Examples
| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| N = 15 | 1 2 Fizz 4 Buzz ... FizzBuzz |
