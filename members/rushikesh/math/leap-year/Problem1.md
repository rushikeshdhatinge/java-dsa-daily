# Leap Year

**Day:** 1
**Topic:** math
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Link:** Classic problem

## Problem
Given a year, check whether it is a leap year and print the result.

## Examples
| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 2024 | Leap |
| 1900 | Not leap |

## Approach
If year is divided 4 and if year is multiple of 100 then divided by 400 then that year consider as leap year

## Complexity
- Time: O(1)
- Space: O(1)
